# FE Copilot demo video v5 (SKO May 2026 - lean edit)

> 3:00 single take. Minimal changes from v4.1. Two surgical edits:
> (1) The 45-second silent autopilot is now 30 seconds with two narration lines - enough to name what the judge is watching without losing the confidence effect.
> (2) The CTA goes from 10 seconds to 30 seconds. Ten seconds was not enough room for the motif to land. The extra time comes from trimming Workspace from 15s to 10s and the autopilot from 45s to 30s.
>
> Everything else in v4.1 is preserved verbatim. If a beat is not listed in the "what changed" table below, use the v4.1 line exactly.

---

## What changed vs v4.1

| Beat | v4.1 | v5 | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| B2 Autopilot | 45 s, fully silent | 30 s, 2 narration lines + pauses | 50 seconds of silence is a risk if the video rendering has any lag or the judge is not watching closely. Two short lines name the two most important moments without taking over. Confidence effect is preserved because the lines are statements, not tour-guide narration. |
| B7 Workspace | 15 s | 10 s | Workspace is the weakest rubric beat. It doesn't win points on its own; it supports Agent Builder and Workflows. 5 seconds saved, no line dropped, one breath removed. |
| B9 CTA | 10 s | 30 s | The six-hours motif resolution needs room. At 10 seconds the line "That's what we just took back" was rushing the most important pause in the video. 30 seconds gives each line its own breath. |
| Timing table | 13 beats at 3:00 | Same 13 beats at 3:00 | The 20 seconds saved in B2 and B7 move entirely into B9. Total runtime unchanged. |
| Pivot line | "Fifty seconds." | "Thirty seconds." | The autopilot now runs 30 seconds, not 50. The pivot line names the actual runtime. Fix this or the judge does the math and notices. |

### B2. Autopilot (0:30 - 1:00, 30 seconds) - CHANGED

```
[CAM A locked wide. Hands flat on the desk, NOT on the keyboard.]
[The autopilot runs: hook, quick-research, brief, Field Assistant, Agent Builder, recap.]

[At 0:42, as the pre-meeting brief renders - say this quietly, without looking at the camera:]

That brief. Grounded in the actual account. In under thirty seconds.

[PAUSE 6 seconds. Small nod when the Field Assistant displacement questions appear.]

[At 0:53, as "Splunk Displacement" lights up in the Agent Builder:]

And it just built the displacement play. Live.

[PAUSE 7 seconds. Lean back as the completion card lands. Do NOT speak again.]
[Let the recap card sit in frame until 1:00.]
```

**Why two lines instead of zero**: The brief rendering and the live agent creation are the two moments the judge must notice. Naming them quietly - without tour-guide energy - is the difference between a screenshare recording and a keynote. The pauses do more work than the lines.

**Why not more than two lines**: The silence is still the speaker. The lines are signposts, not narration.

### B9. CTA (2:30 - 3:00) - EXPANDED to 30 s

```
[CUT to CAM B. Two full beats of silence before speaking. Slight smile.]

Six hours a week.

[PAUSE - count three.]

Every FE. Every week.

[PAUSE - count two.]

That's what we just took back.

[PAUSE - count four. This is the longest pause in the video. Hold the eye contact.]

MIT licensed.

[PAUSE one beat.]

github dot com slash rodrigo dash elastic slash F E dash Elastic.

[PAUSE one beat.]

Take it home.

[Smile lifts.]

[PAUSE two beats.]

Move pilots to real-world impact.

[Hold the eye contact for two full seconds. Do NOT cut early.]
```

**Why 30 seconds**: Each line now gets a pause with a count. "That's what we just took back" is the resolution of the entire motif - it needs four counts, not one. At 10 seconds that pause was physically impossible to hold. At 30 seconds the closeis a real moment, not a scramble.

## 5. What to drill (unchanged from v4.1, with one addition)

- The pause after "Eighteen minutes" should feel almost too long.
- "Took back" is the load-bearing phrase. Land it like you mean it.
- **NEW for v5**: The B9 four-count pause after "That's what we just took back" - rehearse holding it until it is uncomfortable. If it feels comfortable, it is too short. The judge needs to feel the weight before "MIT licensed" lands.
- The URL gets steady eye contact through the camera. No glance at notes.

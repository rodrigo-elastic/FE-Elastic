## 5. 9:16 reframe note

Render two masters: 16:9 (1920x1080) for LinkedIn desktop and the submission form, 9:16 (1080x1920) for Slack mobile and LinkedIn mobile feed.

| Beat | 16:9 framing | 9:16 reframe |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:03 hook (Cam B) | Centered, 3/4 close-up | Native portrait crop. Tight on face, eyes on upper third. Translates well, no rework. |
| 0:03 to 0:15 autopilot (Cam A) | Wide locked | Crop to the iframe panel center. The dashboard hero, FE Brain citations, Battlecards grid, and Industries tiles all render center-aligned, so a center-crop preserves the headline content. Push the caption track to the lower 20% of the portrait frame so it does not collide with the page chrome. |
| 0:15 to 0:23 proof bullets (Cam A) | Wide locked | Center-crop on the stat overlay. Add a 9:16-only large-text overlay layer (1300 / 31 / 12) since the in-page stats are too small to read on phone. |
| 0:23 to 0:30 CTA (Cam B) | Centered, 3/4 close-up | Native portrait crop. URL lower-third must be re-rendered for portrait at a larger font size (44pt minimum). |

Rule: close-ups translate to 9:16 with a center-crop, no creative rework. Wide screen demos need a vertical crop with the iframe panel centered plus an oversized caption layer for legibility. Plan for 30 minutes of extra editing on the 9:16 master.

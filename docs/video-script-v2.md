## 2. Production setup checklist

### Cameras

| | Cam A (master) | Cam B (close-up) |
|---|---|---|
| Position | Centered, lens at eye level, 60 cm away | 30 to 45 degrees off-axis to your strong side, 50 cm away |
| Framing | Medium shot, full face plus shoulders, 1/3 headroom | 3/4 close-up, eyes on the upper third line |
| Lens | 35 to 50 mm equivalent | 50 to 85 mm equivalent (more compression) |
| Resolution | 1080p 30 fps minimum, 4K 30 fps preferred | Same as Cam A |
| White balance | Lock at 5500 K | Lock at 5500 K (match A) |
| Exposure | Manual, expose for the skin (zebras at 70 percent) | Same setting as A |
| Focus | Lock on your eyes, no auto-hunt | Lock on your eyes |
| Sync | Slate clap at start of take, or use timecode if available | Slate clap simultaneously |

Aim to never let Cam B see Cam A in frame. Place Cam B slightly higher than Cam A so the subtle angle change reads as intentional.

### Audio (Shure podcast)

- Position 15 cm (6 inches) from your mouth, off-axis 20 degrees so plosives miss the diaphragm.
- Pop filter or foam windscreen.
- 48 kHz, 24-bit, no built-in noise gate (you can clean up in post).
- Boom arm so the mic stays out of both camera frames.
- Monitor through closed-back headphones during the take so you catch clipping in real time.
- Phone on Do Not Disturb. Mac notifications off (`Focus` mode). Wi-Fi router away from the desk.

### Lighting (Elgato Key Light)

- Key light at 45 degrees above eye level on your strong side.
- Bi-color 4500 to 5500 K to match white balance.
- 30 to 40 percent brightness so the skin is soft, not blasted.
- If you have a second light: fill on the opposite side at 50 percent of key intensity, or bounce off a wall.
- Background: 60 to 90 cm behind you. A small rim or accent light prevents you flatlining into the wall.
- If you wear glasses: tilt the key 5 to 10 degrees further so reflections fall outside the frame.

### Environment

- Soft furnishings to kill reverb (couch, books, blanket on the wall behind the camera).
- Door closed, visitors briefed, AC off if possible (or turn the fan on a low setting and accept the noise floor).
- Browser in fullscreen. Bookmarks bar hidden. Demo banner dismissed (click the x) so the amber strip is gone.
- Pinned tabs (in this order): /, /quick-research.html, /customers.html, /fe-brain.html, /agent-builder.html, /battlecards.html, /industries.html, /workflow-demo.html, /health.html.
- Run the autopilot once before the take so all 9 page iframes are warm.
- Anthropic credits topped up so the live FE Brain query in the close-up actually returns.

## 3. Pre-flight (10 minutes before take 1)

1. Voice warm-up: hum up and down a 5-note scale for 90 seconds. Tongue twisters: "the lips, the teeth, the tip of the tongue" times 5.
2. Hydration: water at room temperature, no dairy, no caffeine in the last 30 minutes.
3. Posture check: feet flat, back tall, chin level, shoulders down.
4. Energy: stand up for 60 seconds, breathe deep, sit down with a smile already on your face. Keep that smile in your eyes through the first 8 seconds.
5. Slate: tap the desk firmly so both cameras and the mic capture a sync transient. Optional verbal slate "FE Copilot, take one, action" 2 seconds before the hook.

## 4. The script (with camera and pacing annotations)

Square brackets are operator cues, do NOT speak them. `[PAUSE]` means stop, breathe, hold.

### B0. Hook (0:00 to 0:08, 18 words)

```
[CAM B. Lean forward 2 cm. Direct eye contact. Hard tone.]

Every Field Engineer at Elastic loses six hours a week to prep,
notes, and follow-up.

[PAUSE one full beat. Hold the eye contact.]

Six. Hours.
```

**Why it works**: Demo2Win opener. Concrete, falsifiable number. The pause + repeat is Apple-style emphasis. Cam B intimacy makes the pain personal before you switch to wide.

### B1. Promise (0:08 to 0:18, 22 words)

```
[CUT to CAM A. Sit back. Hand reaches the trackpad. Smile in your eyes.]

FE Copilot gives those hours back. One click. Forty five seconds.

[Click "Show me the magic 45s" on the dashboard hero.]

Watch.
```

**Why it works**: McKinsey pyramid. Insight first ("hours back"), method second ("one click"). The single word "Watch" forces the audience to commit attention to the silence that follows.

### B2. Autopilot (0:18 to 1:00, 42 seconds, SILENT)

```
[CAM A locked wide. Hands on the desk near the mouse, NOT on the keyboard.]
[The autopilot drives 9 sections. Captions narrate each one.]
[At 0:35: small confident nod when the Industries panel renders.]
[At 0:50: small smile when the recap card lands.]
[Do NOT speak. Do NOT touch the keyboard. The autopilot is the speaker.]
```

**Why it works**: Demo2Win "Tell, Show, Tell". You told them the promise; the autopilot is the show. The silence is uncomfortable for 3 seconds, then mesmerizing. Do not break it.

### B3. Pivot (1:00 to 1:05, 10 words)

```
[CUT to CAM B. Smile lands first. Eye contact one beat before speaking.]

That was every page lighting up.
Now let me show you why each one matters.
```

**Why it works**: Resets attention. Promises specificity (the "why" not the "what").

### B4. FE Brain (1:05 to 1:25, 30 words)

```
[CAM A. Click FE Brain tab.]

FE Brain. Hybrid retrieval over thirteen hundred Elastic doc chunks.
Cited answers in ten seconds.

[Click a chip. Wait for citations to render.]
[CUT to CAM B for the next line.]

Stop pinging Slack.
```

**Why it works**: One stat (1300 chunks), one outcome (10 seconds), one emotional payoff ("Stop pinging Slack"). The Cam B cut on the emotional line is Apple's "one more thing" signature.

### B5. Agent Builder (1:25 to 1:50, 35 words)

```
[CUT back to CAM A. Click Agent Builder tab.]

Agent Builder. Master agent plus three specialists I built.
RFP Responder. Migration Specialist. Compliance Pursuit.

[Click the plus button. The Create dialog opens.]

Click plus, pick tools, give it a system prompt.
It lives in your Kibana cluster, not in this app.

[Close the dialog without saving.]
```

**Why it works**: Rule of 3 in the persona names. The "your Kibana cluster, not in this app" line is the moat: portability, not lock-in.

### B6. Battlecards plus Industries (1:50 to 2:15, 35 words)

```
[CAM A. Click Battlecards tab.]

Thirty one battlecards. Sorted by marketshare.
Splunk, Datadog, CrowdStrike, AWS OpenSearch.

[Click Industries tab.]

Twenty industries. Eighty percent of customers covered.
No starting from scratch.
```

**Why it works**: Two MECE buckets (compete + verticals). Marketshare ordering is the differentiator vs the original alphabetical chaos. "No starting from scratch" closes the value loop.

### B7. Customers Kanban (2:15 to 2:35, 28 words)

```
[CAM A. Click Customers tab.]

Every customer in flight.

[Hover over the Kanban so the customer-color tags are visible.]

Same color across the Kanban means same account.
Filter by stage, search by name, group however helps you think.
```

**Why it works**: The customer-color tag is a small but memorable detail. Mentioning "however helps you think" puts the FE in control, not the tool.

### B8. Workflows (2:35 to 2:50, 22 words)

```
[CUT to CAM B. Slow the cadence by 10 percent.]

Two Kibana workflows close the loop.

[CAM A. Click Workflow tab. Show the 3 cards.]

A transcript drops. The agent runs. Salesforce updates.
The rep does nothing.
```

**Why it works**: Quiet emphasis. The cam cut to B for the headline, cam A for the proof. "The rep does nothing" is a deliberate paradox that lands the autonomy claim.

### B9. CTA (2:50 to 3:00, 22 words)

```
[CUT to CAM B. Slight smile. Hold a one-beat pause before speaking.]

Six hours back. Every Field Engineer. Every week.

[Pause. Eye contact. Half a smile.]

Apache two point oh.
github dot com slash rodrigo dash elastic slash F E dash Elastic.

[Hold the eye contact for 1 full second after the URL. Do NOT cut yet.]

Take it home.
```

**Why it works**: Apple closing pattern. Repeat the headline number. Open-source license = invitation. URL spelled out for clarity. "Take it home" is an active verb that requests action.

## 8. Take strategy

Plan for 3 takes minimum. Block out 60 minutes total.

| Take | Goal | Acceptance criteria |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Warm-up. Expect to throw it away. | Hit all 9 beats, do not stop on small stumbles. |
| 2 | Production take. | All 9 beats clean. Energy holds through the autopilot silence. |
| 3 | Insurance take. | Same as 2 plus optional alt deliveries on the CTA. |

If take 2 nails it, take 3 is optional. If a beat fails twice in the same place, change the line slightly: the brain wants resolution and a new phrasing breaks the loop.

## 9. Post-production sequence

1. Import both Cam files plus the Shure mic file into your editor.
2. Sync to the slate clap. Lock the master clip group.
3. Mute Cam A and Cam B audio. Use the Shure mic as the sole audio track.
4. Apply the camera cut map from Section 5.
5. Color: lift shadows + 5, drop highlights - 5 to recover detail. Match Cam B to Cam A using the white balance picker on a neutral surface.
6. Audio: high-pass filter at 100 Hz, light de-esser if needed, target loudness -16 LUFS integrated.
7. Captions: run through Whisper-large or your editor's speech-to-text, manually clean up, burn in (do NOT rely on YouTube auto-cc since the submission requires a hosted file).
8. Export 1080p H.264 at 10 Mbps, 48 kHz AAC at 256 kbps, mp4 container.
9. Final length must be 3:00 plus or minus 2 seconds.
10. Filename: `FE-Copilot-FY27-SKO-Hackathon-RodrigoCareaga.mp4`.
11. Upload to gDrive, share "anyone at Elastic with the link can view".
12. Paste the link into `docs/submission.md` and the submission form.

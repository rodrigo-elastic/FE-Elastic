Post-meeting

Workflow 2
Orphan action items
High-impact action items with no owner are detected in post-meeting records. Kibana auto-creates a Salesforce task assigned to the account owner.
fec-post-meetings -> Kibana rule -> SFDC task
Workflow 3
Renewal defense
Three or more risk signals on an account trigger the Renewal Defender. Generates a retention play and creates a Salesforce task for the account owner.
3+ risk signals -> Retention play -> SFDC task
